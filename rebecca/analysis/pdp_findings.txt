================================================================================
METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Each PDP shows how predicted quantity changes as one feature varies while
others remain at their mean values. The process:

1. Sample 500 random test samples
2. For each feature, create 20 different values across its range
3. At each value, replace that feature and get model predictions
4. Average predictions across all samples
5. Plot the trend line showing the relationship
